# Knowledge Taxonomy — Minister's Office

This deck defines the top-level metadata vocabulary reused across the
knowledge base. Twelve categories organise every record.

## CM Meetings
Meetings chaired by the Chief Minister. Includes the review meeting on
semiconductor manufacturing investment held with the Industries department,
and periodic infrastructure review meetings.

## Announcements
Public announcements and inaugurations tracked by the office, including the
Quantum technology park announcement and the state data centre policy.

## Cabinet Decisions
Decisions taken by the state cabinet (मंत्रिमंडळ). Includes the cabinet
decision to adopt the SAMAGRA (समग्र) unified beneficiary database across
welfare departments.

## War Room
War room monitoring of priority projects — water supply (पाणीपुरवठा)
missions, metro corridors and flagship infrastructure.

## 100/150 Days
Deliverables tracked against the government's 100-day and 150-day programmes.

## CEGIS
Work streams run in partnership with CEGIS on state capability and outcome
measurement.

## SAMAGRA
The SAMAGRA (समग्र) unified data and governance programme; beneficiary
deduplication and single source of truth for welfare schemes.

## Databases
Consolidated datasets and registers maintained by departments.

## Conferences
Summits, conferences and परिषद hosted or attended by the office, including
the state investment summit.

## Key Districts
Priority districts under focused monitoring (जिल्हे), including aspirational
district programmes.

## PS Meetings
Meetings convened by Principal Secretaries (प्रधान सचिव) for departmental
coordination.

## Uncategorised
Catch-all for records that rule-based tagging cannot confidently place; kept
visible so nothing is silently lost.
