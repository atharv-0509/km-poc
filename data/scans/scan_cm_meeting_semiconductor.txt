मुख्यमंत्री बैठक — इतिवृत्त / MINUTES OF CM MEETING
Subject: Semiconductor manufacturing investment review
दिनांक / Date: 19 April 2025
Venue: Sahyadri Guest House, Mumbai

Present: Hon'ble Chief Minister, Principal Secretary (Industries),
Secretary (IT), representatives of the semiconductor consortium.

Discussion:
1. The Chief Minister reviewed progress on the अर्धवाहक (semiconductor)
   manufacturing project proposed near Dhule-Nashik. Land acquisition of
   approximately 400 acres is under way.
2. Central government support has been sought for the सेमीकंडक्टर fabrication
   unit; a letter to the Prime Minister has been dispatched.
3. Skilling: a dedicated semiconductor manufacturing skilling centre to be
   set up with Higher & Technical Education department.

Decision: Industries department to submit a detailed project report within
30 days. Next review meeting to be chaired by the Chief Minister.
