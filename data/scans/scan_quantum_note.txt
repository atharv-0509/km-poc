NOTE FOR FILE — QUANTUM MISSION
क्वांटम तंत्रज्ञान अभियान — टिपण्णी

The state proposes to establish a Quantum technology park (क्वांटम तंत्रज्ञान
उद्यान) at Pune in partnership with academic institutions. A Quantum research
centre and startup incubator are envisaged.

Key points:
- Quantum computing and quantum communication research to be prioritised.
- Estimated outlay to be finalised by the Industries department.
- A review meeting on the Quantum mission (क्वांटम मिशन) is to be convened by
  the Chief Secretary; an outward letter has been issued.

Category: Announcements. Department: Industries. Location: Pune.
