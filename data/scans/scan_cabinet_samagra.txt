मंत्रिमंडळ निर्णय / CABINET DECISION
विषय / Subject: Adoption of SAMAGRA unified beneficiary database
दिनांक / Date: 12 March 2025

The state cabinet (मंत्रिमंडळ) approved the adoption of the SAMAGRA (समग्र)
unified beneficiary database across all welfare departments.

Decisions:
1. SAMAGRA to serve as the single source of truth for welfare scheme
   beneficiaries; deduplication of records across departments.
2. General Administration department designated as the nodal department.
3. Databases from Health, Agriculture and Rural Development to be integrated
   into SAMAGRA in a phased manner.

This cabinet decision supports the office's data governance programme.
